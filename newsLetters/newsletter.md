## Advancements in AI

- **How human movement predicts health measures across multiple body systems** — Newton Tech, a developer of AI-powered movement intelligence technology for clinical research, has published a new study it co-authored in Nature Medicine showing that artificial intelligence can reveal extensive health information encoded in subtle patterns of human movement. In 3,328 adults, the model identified significant associations with 1,887 of 3,210 health measures spanning 18 body systems, from blood tests and liver imaging to sleep, mental health and clinical diagnoses. ([Medical Xpress](https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-11-human-movement-health-multiple-body.html), 2026-11-10T03:20:01Z)
